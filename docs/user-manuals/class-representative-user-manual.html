Class Representative — User Manual1 · About This Manual

1About This Manual

1.1Purpose and audience

The Class Representative (CR) login gives one student per section a read-only view of their own class timetable inside the College Management System.

This manual is written for two readers. The Class Representative will find how to sign in and use the two screens available to them. The Head of Department or College Office staff member responsible for the section will find how to create, reset and remove the login.

The role is deliberately small. A Class Representative can see their section's schedule and nothing else — they cannot mark attendance, view the student roster, apply for permissions or see marks. Section 2 sets out exactly what the role can and cannot do.

1.2How to use this guide

Sections 1 to 3 explain the role and the terms used. Section 4 is for the person creating the login. Sections 5 to 8 are the day-to-day instructions for the Class Representative. Sections 9 to 11 provide the end-to-end workflow, the rules that constrain the role, and a troubleshooting table.

Throughout, the exact words shown on screen are set in bold — for example, select Full Timetable. Web addresses and technical values appear as code.

Note

Your college may hide individual menu entries. If a screen described here does not appear in your sidebar, it has been switched off for your college rather than removed from the system.

1.3Roles covered

RoleShown in system asInvolvement
Class Representative Class Leader Signs in and uses the Dashboard, Class Timetable and profile screens.
Head of Department Head of Department Creates the login from the section record, resets its password, removes it.
College Office / Principal College Admin May perform the same actions as the Head of Department for any section.
Important

The Class Representative login is separate from a student's own roll-number login. A student who has both signs in with different credentials for each; the two accounts do not share data.

2.2Access restrictions

  • A Class Representative login is bound to exactly one section, fixed when the login is created.
  • The system serves only that section. A request for any other section is ignored — the restriction is enforced on the server, not merely hidden in the menu.
  • The role has no access to any other part of the system. Addresses outside /class-leader are blocked.
  • A section may have one Class Representative login at a time. To change holder, remove the existing login and create a new one.
Warning

The Class Representative is a shared, rotating position. Treat the password as belonging to the post rather than the person: whenever the holder changes, the Head of Department should reset the password or remove and recreate the login.

Class Representative — User Manual3 · Key Concepts

3Key Concepts

3.1Terms used in this manual

TermMeaning
Class Representative (CR) The student appointed to represent a section. In the system the role is labelled Class Leader; the two names refer to the same login.
Section One class group — for example CSE-A — belonging to a course, a year of study and an admission batch. The CR login is tied to one of these.
Class Incharge The faculty member responsible for the section. Shown on the Dashboard as In-charge: followed by their name.
Period One teaching slot in the day, numbered in sequence. Its start and end times come from the college's semester timings.
Substitute A faculty member covering a period for the regular teacher, usually because of approved leave. Shown as Sub: followed by their name.
Lab batch A subdivision of the section for practical classes. A period may apply to one batch only.
Semester The teaching term. Timetables are kept per semester, and the CR may look at any semester their course has.

3.2Prerequisites

Before a Class Representative can sign in and see a timetable, all of the following must already exist.

PrerequisiteSet up byIf missing
The section recordHead of Department There is nothing to bind the login to.
The student on the section rosterCollege Office The student cannot be chosen in Select Student (CR).
Semester timings for the course and yearCollege Office The timetable shows Timings Not Configured.
A published timetable for the sectionHead of Department or Timetable Incharge The schedule appears empty even though sign-in succeeds.
Note

The Class Representative sees the timetable exactly as published. Any correction — a changed period, a room, a substitute — is made by the Head of Department or Timetable Incharge and appears to the CR the next time the page is loaded.

Class Representative — User Manual4 · Creating the Login

4Creating the Class Representative Login

Who performs this: Head of Department, College Office or Principal.

Before you start: confirm the section exists and the student is on its roster, and decide the email address and temporary password to issue.

4.1Create the login

  1. From the sidebar select Sections.
  2. Open the section the representative belongs to, then select Edit.
  3. Scroll to the Class Leader Login panel.
  4. Under Select Student (CR), choose the student from Enrolled Students. The list contains only students in this section.
  5. Enter an Email. This becomes the username the representative signs in with.
  6. Enter a Temporary Password of at least six characters.
  7. Select Set Login.

On success the message Class Leader account created is shown and the panel changes to display the holder's name with Reset Password and Remove actions. Issue the email address and temporary password to the student and ask them to change the password at first sign-in (section 8.2).

4.2Validation messages

MessageMeaning and what to do
Email and password are both required One of the two boxes is empty. Complete both and select Set Login again.
Password must be at least 6 characters Choose a longer temporary password.
Failed to create Class Leader login The account could not be created — most often because the email address is already in use by another login. Use a different address.
Important

The email address is the username. It does not have to be a college address, but it must not already belong to another account in the system.

4.3Reset the password

Use this when a representative has forgotten their password, or when the position passes to another student and you want to keep the same login.

  1. Open Sections → the section → Edit.
  2. In the Class Leader Login panel select Reset Password.
  3. In the Reset Class Leader Password dialog, type the New Password (at least six characters).
  4. Confirm the dialog.

On success the message Password reset is shown. The previous password stops working immediately. If the reset fails, Failed to reset password is shown and the old password remains in force.

4.4Remove the login

Use this when the section no longer needs a representative, or when you intend to issue the login to a different student under a different email address.

  1. Open Sections → the section → Edit.
  2. In the Class Leader Login panel select Remove.
  3. Confirm when prompted.

On success the message Class Leader login removed is shown. The account can no longer sign in and the section is left with no representative until a new login is created.

Warning

Removal takes effect immediately and cannot be undone from this screen. To restore access you must create a new login using the steps in section 4.1.

Note

Removing the login does not affect the student's own record, their roll-number login, or anything on the section. Only the representative account is withdrawn.

Choosing between reset and remove

SituationRecommended action
Password forgottenReset Password — the same holder keeps the account.
New representative, same email acceptableReset Password, then reissue the credentials.
New representative, own email wantedRemove, then create a new login (4.1).
Section no longer needs a CRRemove.

5Getting Started as a Class Representative

5.1Signing in

Who performs this: Class Representative. Before you start: you need the email address and password issued by your Head of Department.

  1. Open the College Management System in your browser.
  2. In Username, type the email address you were given.
  3. In Password, type your password. Select the eye icon (Show password) to check what you have typed.
  4. Select Sign in.

The button shows Signing in… while it works. On success you arrive at your Dashboard, headed with your section's name.

Important

Sign in with the email address issued for the Class Representative post — not with your roll number. The sign-in box is labelled Email or Roll Number because it is shared with students and staff, but a roll number will not open the representative account.

5.3Navigating the dashboard

After signing in you see the sidebar on the left and the current screen on the right. The Class Representative sidebar has three entries and no others.

What the top of the Dashboard tells you

The heading confirms which section the login is bound to. Beneath it, a single line lists:

Department
The department the section belongs to.
Batch
The admission batch, for example 2024–2028.
In-charge
The faculty member responsible for the section, when one is assigned.

If the heading reads Class Leader Dashboard rather than your section's name, and the page shows No Section Linked, your login has not been bound to a section — see section 11.

Note

Signing out is done from your account menu at the top right of the screen. Your session ends automatically after 24 hours, after which you sign in again.

5.4What to do on your first sign-in

Work through these four steps once, when you first receive the login.

  1. Sign in with the email address and temporary password you were given (section 5.1).
  2. Open My Profile and select Change Password. Replace the temporary password with one only you know (section 8.2).
  3. Check the Dashboard heading names your own section, and that the In-charge shown is your Class Incharge. If either is wrong, report it before relying on the schedule.
  4. Open Timetable and confirm the week shown matches the classes you are attending. If the timetable is empty, see section 11.

Class Representative — User Manual6 · Using the Dashboard

6Using the Dashboard

6.1Class Schedule

The Class Schedule panel lists the periods for one day. Today's day is shown first; the line beneath the heading names the day being displayed.

When the timetable runs on more than one day, a row of day buttons appears at the top right of the panel. Select a day to switch to it. Only days that actually have classes are offered.

What each period card shows

ElementMeaning
Period nThe period number within the day.
LabShown when the period is a practical class.
Subject nameThe subject taught in that period. A lab batch, when the period applies to one, follows the subject name.
TimeThe start and end time, taken from the college's semester timings.
Faculty nameThe teacher for that period, or Not assigned when none has been set.
Sub: nameA substitute is covering the period. Highlighted so it is easy to spot.
RoomThe classroom, when one is recorded for the period.
Note

If a day has no classes, the panel reads No classes scheduled for that day. This is normal for a day the section does not attend.

Opening the full week

Select Full Timetable at the top right of the Dashboard to open the weekly view described in section 7.

6.2Semester subjects

Below the schedule, a second panel lists the subjects running in the current semester — headed, for example, III Sem Subjects. For each subject it shows the subject code, the subject name, whether it is a lab, and the faculty assigned to it.

This list is drawn from the teaching assignments made by the Head of Department. If it reads No subjects or faculty are assigned for this semester yet, the assignments have not been completed — this is a matter for the department, not something the representative can change.

Keeping the information current

Both panels are built from live records each time the page loads. There is no separate refresh action: reload the page to pick up a change made by the department — a newly assigned faculty member, a substitute arranged for approved leave, or a corrected room.

Important

A substitute shown against a period reflects an approved leave arrangement already recorded in the system. Treat it as the authoritative teacher for that period unless your department tells you otherwise.

7Using the Class Timetable

7.1Opening the timetable

Select Timetable in the sidebar, or Full Timetable on the Dashboard. The page is headed Class Timetable and shows your section's weekly schedule.

Two messages may appear instead of a timetable:

  • No Section Linked — your login is not bound to a section. Contact your College Office or Head of Department.
  • Timings Not Configured — semester timings have not been set for your course and year, so periods have no times yet. This is set up by the College Office.

7.2Filters and views

The controls above the timetable let you narrow and change what is displayed.

ControlWhat it does
Semester: chips Switches between the semesters your course has. The current semester is selected when the page opens.
Week navigation Moves to the previous or next week. Today returns to the current week.
All / Theory / Practical Shows all periods, theory periods only, or practical periods only.
Batch: All Appears only when the section has lab batches. Choose All Batches or a single batch.
Clear filters Appears once a filter is active; returns the view to all periods and all batches.
Week Matrix / Day View Switches between the full-week grid and a single day at a time.
Note

Filters change only what you see. They do not alter the timetable, and they are not saved — the page opens unfiltered next time.

Breaks appear in the grid as Lunch Break and Short Break. A period with no class shows as Free, and a period awaiting a teacher shows as Not assigned yet. During a live class the current period is marked LIVE.

7.3Download, export and print

The timetable can be taken away in three formats — useful for pinning on a notice board or sharing with the class.

ActionResult
Download PDF Saves the timetable as a PDF laid out for printing. The message Timetable downloaded confirms success.
Export Excel Saves the timetable as a spreadsheet. The message Timetable exported confirms success.
Print Opens your browser's print dialog with the timetable formatted for paper.

If an export does not work

MessageWhat to do
Download failedTry again. If it persists, use Print and save as PDF from the print dialog.
Export failedTry again, or use Download PDF instead.
Popup blockedYour browser blocked the print window. Allow pop-ups for this site and select Print again.
Important

A downloaded or printed timetable is a snapshot. If the department later changes a period or arranges a substitute, your copy will be out of date — take a fresh one after any announced change.

What the exported copy contains

The exported timetable carries the college name, your course, year, section and semester, and the period grid as displayed — including breaks and room numbers. Any filter you have applied is reflected in the export, so clear filters first if you want the complete week.

Class Representative — User Manual8 · Managing Your Profile

8Managing Your Profile

Select My Profile in the sidebar. The page is headed My Profile, described as Manage your profile photo and account details.

8.1Profile photo

Your photo appears beside your name across the system. Use the photo control at the top of the page to upload a new picture or replace the existing one.

Beneath it, your account details are listed for reference — your name, the section your login is bound to, and your role. These are set by your Head of Department and cannot be edited here.

8.2Changing your password

When to do this: immediately after your first sign-in with the temporary password, and whenever you believe the password may be known to others.

  1. Select Change Password at the top right of the profile page.
  2. In the Set a new password dialog, type your current password.
  3. Type the new password. It must be at least 8 characters — the box notes Min 8 characters.
  4. Select Change Password to confirm, or Cancel to abandon.

On success the message Password changed is shown. Use the new password from then on.

MessageWhat to do
Current password is incorrect.Re-enter your existing password.
New password is too weak — use at least 8 characters.Choose a longer password.
Please log out and log back in, then try again.Your session is too old to change a password. Sign out, sign in and retry.
Too many attempts. Please try again later.Wait a few minutes before trying again.
Warning

Do not share the Class Representative password with classmates. If the position changes hands, ask your Head of Department to reset it rather than passing the password on.

Class Representative — User Manual10 · Rules and Limitations

10Important Rules and Limitations

10.1Scope of access

  • The login is bound to one section, chosen when it is created. The binding cannot be changed from the representative's own screens.
  • The system ignores any attempt to request a different section and always returns the bound one. This is enforced on the server.
  • Addresses outside /class-leader are blocked for this role.

10.2What the role cannot do

  • Mark, edit or view student attendance.
  • View the student roster, personal student details, marks or internal exam results.
  • Raise or approve student permission requests.
  • Edit the timetable, assign faculty, or arrange a substitute.
  • See another section, another year or another department.
  • Create, reset or remove any login, including its own.

10.3Data dependencies

If this is missingThe representative sees
Section binding on the loginNo Section Linked on both screens
Semester timings for the course and yearTimings Not Configured
Published timetable periodsAn empty schedule, with no error
Teaching assignments for the semesterNo subjects or faculty are assigned for this semester yet
Faculty on an individual periodNot assigned against that period

10.4Account rules

  • One Class Representative login per section at a time.
  • The email address is the username and must be unique across the whole system.
  • A temporary password set by the Head of Department must be at least 6 characters; a password the representative sets for themselves must be at least 8.
  • A session lasts 24 hours, after which signing in again is required.
Warning

Removing the login takes effect at once and cannot be undone. Restoring access means creating a new login from the section record.
